🌕 **Proud Moment for MOON Lab!** 🚀

We are delighted to share that **Vinita Sao** has received the Best Poster Award at IEEE MRS 2025 for her work on:
📌 *Heterogeneous Multi-Robot Decentralized Task Allocation with Fuel Constraints*

Her work brings forward a highly relevant direction in multi-robot autonomy – addressing fuel constraints, modelling heterogeneous capabilities, and proposing a decentralized algorithm that achieves near-optimal performance with much faster computation. A powerful step towards scalable, real-world multi-robot systems. 🤖⚡

A huge congratulations to Vinita for this achievement and for showcasing the lab’s work at an international platform. 👏

A special appreciation to advisors Sujit P B and Sujoy Bhore, and all lab members whose discussions, collaboration, and support strengthened this research.

We’re excited to keep pushing boundaries in multi-agent intelligence and autonomy — and this milestone motivates us even more. 🚀

Onwards to more breakthroughs! 🔭
